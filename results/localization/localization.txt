Numerical check of Theorem 2.3: the contact dissipation of the penalized
solution concentrates in an O(eps) layer along the entry portion of the
contact boundary, vanishes in the interior of the contact set, and puts
exactly nothing on the detachment front.

=== Example 1 (uniform impact, triangular contact set) ===
parameters      : l=1 T=0.3 dx=0.0002 dt=0.0002 alpha=0.01 eps=0.0005 h=1 N=5000 M=1500 dt/eps=0.4
  dissipating cells               : 241520 (3.22% of the (t, x) grid)
  all inside {eta<0} with v<0     : True (exact, by construction of P)
  cells in contact with v >= 0    : 3609657 -- carry zero dissipation (the detachment front dissipates nothing, cf. (A3)/(2.14))
  penetration depth               : 2.557e-02 (51.1 eps)
  50% of dissipation within      : 2.000e-04 of the boundary (0.40 eps, 1.0 cells)
  95% of dissipation within      : 8.000e-04 of the boundary (1.60 eps, 4.0 cells)
  99% of dissipation within      : 1.000e-03 of the boundary (2.00 eps, 5.0 cells)

=== Example 3 (rolling contact front) ===
parameters      : l=1 T=0.7 dx=0.0002 dt=0.0002 alpha=0.01 eps=0.0005 h=1 N=5000 M=3500 dt/eps=0.4
  dissipating cells               : 360056 (2.06% of the (t, x) grid)
  all inside {eta<0} with v<0     : True (exact, by construction of P)
  cells in contact with v >= 0    : 2731433 -- carry zero dissipation (the detachment front dissipates nothing, cf. (A3)/(2.14))
  penetration depth               : 2.792e-02 (55.8 eps)
  50% of dissipation within      : 2.000e-04 of the boundary (0.40 eps, 1.0 cells)
  95% of dissipation within      : 8.246e-04 of the boundary (1.65 eps, 4.1 cells)
  99% of dissipation within      : 1.414e-03 of the boundary (2.83 eps, 7.1 cells)

d95 shrinking proportionally to eps (d95/eps roughly constant) is the
discrete counterpart of the dissipation concentrating on the boundary
as eps -> 0.
